HANDWAVE (CUSTOMIZABLE EDITION)
Control your laptop with hand gestures, using the webcam.

START
  Just double-click HandWave.exe!
  A window opens showing your camera with the hand skeleton overlay.

TWO-HAND SAFETY CLUTCH (PREVENTS ACCIDENTAL MISTAKES)
  To prevent unintended gestures while talking, drinking coffee, or moving your hands,
  the Safety Clutch lets you require a guard sign from one hand before gestures can fire!

  How it works:
    - Left Hand (Guard Hand): Holds Open Palm (✋), Peace Sign (✌️), or Fist (✊).
    - Right Hand (Action Hand): Performs Pinches, Pointing, Volume Up/Down.
    - When Guard Hand is NOT showing: Screen shows [LOCKED 🔒]. Zero gestures can fire.
    - When Guard Hand is showing: Screen shows [ARMED 🟢]. Gestures work immediately!

    Quick Toggle:
    - Press 'C' anytime while the camera is on to toggle the Safety Clutch ON/OFF!
    - Or configure Clutch Sign & Mode in the Settings Window.

DOUBLE-GESTURE CONFIRMATION (DOUBLE-TAP TO FIRE)
  Beside the Two-Hand Safety Clutch, you can enable Double-Gesture Confirmation
  to completely eliminate false triggers using only ONE hand!

  How it works:
    - Tap 1: Perform any gesture (e.g., pinch, point, or wave). The screen prompts:
      [DOUBLE-TAP: Repeat gesture within 0.7s to fire] with an amber countdown bar.
    - Tap 2: Release and perform the same gesture again within the window.
    - Result: The action fires!
    - If you do not perform the second gesture within the window, it safely cancels.
    - Ideal for drinking coffee, talking, or presenting with zero accidental clicks!

  Quick Toggle:
    - Press 'D' anytime while the camera window is open to toggle Double-Tap ON/OFF!
    - Or toggle "Double-Gesture Confirmation" in the Settings Window ('S' key).

SPLIT HANDS MODE (SEPARATE LEFT & RIGHT HAND CONTROLS)
  You can configure independent actions for your Left and Right hands!
  - When Split Hands is ON:
      * Left Hand is color-coded in Cyan / Light Blue and acts as your Media & Multitasking pad.
      * Right Hand is color-coded in Emerald Green and acts as your Navigation & Main control.
  - When Split Hands is OFF (Unified Mode):
      * Both hands share the same unified gesture actions (standard mode).

  How to Toggle Split Hands ON / OFF:
    - Press 'H' anytime while the camera window is open to instantly toggle Split Hands ON/OFF!
    - Or toggle "Split Hands Mode" checkbutton in the Settings Window ('S' key).

SETTINGS & CUSTOMIZATION
  You can customize any gesture, turn off gestures you don't need, or change
  the keyboard/mouse action assigned to each gesture!

  How to open Settings:
    - Press 'S' on your keyboard while the HandWave camera window is open.
    - Or double-click Open_Settings.bat anytime.

  In the Settings Window:
    - Double-Gesture Confirmation: Toggle ON/OFF and adjust confirmation window slider (0.4s - 1.3s).
    - Split Hands Mode: Toggle ON/OFF to configure Left and Right hands separately via tabs.
    - Two-Hand Safety Clutch: Enable/disable and choose Guard Sign & Mode.
    - Active Gestures & Mappings: Check/Uncheck any gesture and choose its action:
        * Desktop & Multitasking: Switch Desktop Right/Left (Win+Ctrl+Arrows),
          Task View (Win+Tab), Show Desktop / Minimize All (Win+D), Switch App (Alt+Tab)
        * Window Management: Maximize (Win+Up), Minimize (Win+Down), Close Window (Alt+F4)
        * Media & Volume: Volume Up/Down/Mute, Play/Pause, Next/Prev Track
        * Navigation: Right/Left/Up/Down Arrow, Page Up/Down, Space, Enter, Escape
        * Mouse: Left Click, Right Click, or None.
    - Adjust the "Hold Duration" slider to change gesture sensitivity.
    - Click "Save & Apply" to apply changes immediately without restarting.

QUICK KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS IN CAMERA WINDOW
  C ......... Toggle Safety Clutch ON / OFF
  D ......... Toggle Double-Gesture Confirmation ON / OFF (Double-Tap to fire)
  H ......... Toggle Split Hands Mode ON / OFF (Independent Left & Right Hands)
  S ......... Open the Settings Window
  P ......... Pause or resume gesture detection
  1 ......... Quick toggle Thumbs Up on/off
  2 ......... Quick toggle Thumbs Down on/off
  3 ......... Quick toggle Point Right on/off
  4 ......... Quick toggle Point Left on/off
  5 ......... Quick toggle Pinch (Thumb + Index) on/off
  6 ......... Quick toggle Pinch (Thumb + Middle) on/off
  7 ......... Quick toggle Pinch (Thumb + Ring) on/off
  8 ......... Quick toggle Pinch (Thumb + Pinky) on/off
  Q or Esc .. Quit application

GESTURES (DEFAULT MAPPINGS)
  [When Split Hands is OFF - Unified Mode]:
    Thumbs up ................... Volume Up    (repeats while held)
    Thumbs down ................. Volume Down  (repeats while held)
    Point right (index) ......... Right Arrow  (next slide / seek)
    Point left (index) .......... Left Arrow   (prev slide / seek)
    Pinch (Thumb + Index) ....... Page Down / Space
    Pinch (Thumb + Middle) ...... Page Up / Enter
    Pinch (Thumb + Ring) ........ Home
    Pinch (Thumb + Pinky) ....... Volume Mute / End
    Swipe Right (Wave Right) .... Right Arrow
    Swipe Left (Wave Left) ...... Left Arrow
    Swipe Up (Wave Up) .......... Page Up
    Swipe Down (Wave Down) ...... Page Down

  [When Split Hands is ON - Independent Profiles]:
    🟢 Right Hand (Navigation & Main Actions):
      - Thumbs Up / Down ........ Volume Up / Down
      - Point Right / Left ...... Right Arrow / Left Arrow
      - Pinch Thumb+Index ....... Page Down (Scroll Down)
      - Pinch Thumb+Middle ...... Page Up (Scroll Up)
      - Pinch Thumb+Ring ........ Home
      - Pinch Thumb+Pinky ....... End
      - Swipe Right / Left ...... Right Arrow / Left Arrow
      - Swipe Up / Down ......... Page Up / Page Down

    🔵 Left Hand (Media & Multitasking Actions):
      - Thumbs Up / Down ........ Volume Up / Down
      - Point Right / Left ...... Next Track / Previous Track
      - Pinch Thumb+Index ....... Space bar (Play / Pause)
      - Pinch Thumb+Middle ...... Volume Mute
      - Pinch Thumb+Ring ........ Task View (Win+Tab)
      - Pinch Thumb+Pinky ....... Show Desktop (Win+D)
      - Swipe Right / Left ...... Switch Virtual Desktop Right / Left
      - Swipe Up / Down ......... Task View / Show Desktop


GOOD TO KNOW
  - Visual Feedback: The camera HUD displays [RIGHT HAND] or [LEFT HAND] next to gestures,
    and colors the skeleton green (Right) or cyan (Left).
  - Arrow keys, Enter, and Space go to whichever window you clicked last. Click your
    presentation, video or browser window once after starting HandWave.
  - If it says the camera cannot be opened, close other apps using the webcam
    (Zoom, Teams, Camera app) and check privacy settings.
  - Good lighting helps tracking. Keep your hands inside the webcam frame.

PRIVACY
  All camera images and hand landmarks are processed 100% locally on your PC.
  Nothing is recorded, saved or transmitted, and no internet connection is used.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a webcam.
